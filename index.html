Shipping

How it gets to you

We do not keep stock in a warehouse here yet. When you order, we pass it to our supplier and they post it your way. Most Australian parcels land in about 1 to 2 weeks, and you get tracking once it leaves.

Free shipping over A$60. Under that it is a flat A$8.95. Customs can be slow sometimes, so we would rather say that upfront than pretend otherwise.
